## 1. Summary

Campus Quarium is a mobile web app for reporting campus problems to the office that can fix them. A student sends a report with a photo as proof. Other students with the same problem co-sign it. When enough students have co-signed, the app emails one summary to that office with a link to reply.

The goals are that students reach the right office without a student leader carrying the report, staff get one email per problem instead of many, the app shows one count of affected students, and students see status changes without asking.

## 2. Problem

Students can go through student leaders. If students don't trust the leader, or the leader doesn't want to offend management, the leader delays or softens the report. The student also has no proof that the leader passed the message on.

Students can also post on WhatsApp, but unit heads will not give their numbers to 13,000 students. Hundreds of other messages in a hall or department group bury the post before the person who can act on it sees it.

## 3. User

Students sign in with their school email, file reports, and co-sign. Most will arrive via a WhatsApp link on a phone.

Unit heads and their staff only receive email and reply through a link.

## 4. Risks

* Management thinks the app is against them: Present it as a filter for duplicate complaints and leave out timers or blame.
* Student leaders feel bypassed: Do not present it as a replacement for the union. Start with unit heads before approaching the senate.
* Staff ignore the email: Send one email with a one-use reply link so staff need no account.
